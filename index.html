How Squeekchain powers this game

  • Every ~2 seconds the game polls the public API at api.squeekchain.com/v1/status — the same feed the official site uses.
  • Gameplay is gated by the real wheel: live cheese spawns only while coreStatus says Squeektoshi is running.
  • Naps can be long, so during them the game switches to a clearly labelled Dream Mode: translucent practice cheese at a gentle fixed tempo that never pretends to be his wheel. Dream catches are scored separately and missing one costs no life — your real score only ever comes from the real wheel.
  • His speedMmPerSecond becomes the falling speed; his rpmMilli becomes the spawn tempo — and spins the wheel icon above at his true RPM.
  • The strip under the game shows his settled block, round and lifetime distance, straight from the same response.
  • If the feed is unreachable or stale, the game says so and pauses. It never fakes live data.

Squeek Dash is a community project for the Squeekchain Buildooooor badge. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Squeekchain team. No wallet, no tokens, no transactions — Squeekchain is a Sepolia testnet project and nothing here has monetary value.
